*Working Title: Empire of Dreams*

*Premise from notes: Man scorns the only woman he truly ever loved because of the pain in his own heart and his search for meaning in his own life. Only after he pushes her away does he realize his mistake but it’s now impossible for him to go back to her. Only in his dreams is he happy, where they can still be together and he gets to be close to the daughter they never had. He creates a dream machine so he can perpetually live the life he threw away, but something about it was made incorrectly, so now all the people that live in nearby towns only experience his same dream every time they sleep (they all are in the dream together and all remember the same dream and notice one another in it). Arcane cleaners are brought to help, and are completely baffled how to find this man and his machine in real life. They are only able to fix the situation in this shared dream world by talking to the sad man and pleading/forcing him to choose the pain of reality for the chance to build something new and good rather than wallow in loss.*

## Outline
* getting their footing
	* The team arrives to the town, and takes stock of the situation
	* They know the issue, have a clear idea of what they need to do, and who they're looking for, but no one has an idea of where he is.
	* The whole town is sleepless as they can't stand the sorrow they feel experiencing someone else's sad dream every night. They feel what he feels in his dreams, and are haunted by the feelings of happiness and loss.
* Finding the guy
	* Kitt and Skjold stay in town, and both have the same dream that the townspeople have been complaining about
	* In the morning they corroborate stories and see if there were any clues in the dream on where to find the guy
	* They search the surrounding area, and by talking to the towns people, they realize that some of them in different areas have not been having the dreams. Mapping who has and hasn't been having the bad dream allows them to triangulate where the guy is located in a wide radius
* looking for an audience with the emperor
	* They find the old house the guy is staying in, but they're having trouble reaching him. (maybe doors are just locked, maybe the doors are open, but guy is in an unwakeable state) They notice a large statue outside of a moth, but it's antenna are made of a different material, big half moon shapes with threads and beads woven in them, like a dream catcher split in two.
	* They wait outside the house so long trying to talk/find the guy they end up falling asleep in the van or maybe inside the house
	* There they finally see the guy, and begin to try and reach/talk to him, but don't realize that they're in his dream
	* guy realizes there are people trying to talk to him, sees who they are, and tries to evade/thwart them. Things start to become surreal as the guy lucid dreams and the two heros quickly realize they're in guy's dream not reality. 
	* they wake up and guy isn't there/was never there. they realize the best chance they have to talk to him is to find him again in the dream.
* walking the tightrope
	* the stag and Kitt decide to try and pinch the guy between the waking and dreaming worlds. Kitt will enter the dream while the Stag tries to find the guy and the machine responsible for broadcasting the dream to the surrounding area
	* Kitt finds the guy in the dream and gives chase, trying to get him to stop and talk to her. Skjold uses tech stuff to find the source of the broadcast. (maybe high freq sound analyzer? the dreams are transmitted in a strange way that can be picked up by an unexpected machine? Maybe the moth statue antenna give the secret away when they broadcast...)
    * Stag finds the control room (possibly under the statue via secret entrance, but possibly better would be in a hidden shack in the corner of the property.)
    * Looking at the control panel and analysing the frequencies with his machine, stag starts to understand how the broadcast works, and using his gizmo broadcasts his voice into the dream, letting kitt know he's found the source with his machine and is going to go figure out how to shut it off. Skjold's words boom in the shared dream where both guy and Kitt can hear it.
    * guy is shocked to hear Stag's voice, in a desperate attempt to stop Kitt and Stag's plan, pushes her into a pit of unwaking sleep. As Kitt falls, Guy's one love in the dream, radiating light, glides in and pulls Kitt out of the pit as she falls. Stopping him from hurting Kitt. Realizing what he almost did, he gives up, and wakes both himself and Kitt.
* The emperor removes his crown
	* Kitt wakes in the van/house, and Stag tells her to join him in the control room. She walks in to see Stag leaning against a wall, and guy slumped in his chair crying, with grief and resignation, at the control panel.
    * Kitt asks why he's doing this to the towns people, guy explains he left the love of his life to pursue his dream doing something bigger than himself, of creating this device, allowing anyone to share their dreams with anyone else, but he hasn't been able to figure out how to get it to transmit anyone else's dreams but his own. He confesses that he realized his mistake of leaving his love, but his love refuses to take him back, and has started moving on without him. And to his despair, now seems happier without him.
    * He now wallows in his own self pity, choosing to stay in the dream of the life he could have had with his love, but threw away due to his own misguided ambitions.
	* Stag explains that they've come to disable/destory the machine as it's become a major problem for people in the surrounding area.
	* Guy crumples and begins to sob, begging the Stag to not destroy his machine, as it's the only thing left that validates the life choices he's made. Kitt pleads with guy turn off the machine, and to deal with his loss, and move forward. The Stag, just now learning about why this whole mess occurs, agrees with Kitt, but bust's guy's chops, scolding him for thinking he could escape reality, and dragging others into his bullshit.
	* guy quietly crys and walks away from the machine, telling the Stag how to turn the machine off. Guy sits down at the end of the room, and Kitt comes up to console him. After disabling the machine, in a rare moment of tenderness, the Stag offers to pulls some strings, and get (new character: Samson, it's just brainiac Sam Ruben) to come and help guy finish his prototype, akwardly mentioning something like "you're down but not out" then leaves for the van.
