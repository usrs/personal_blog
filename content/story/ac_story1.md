## Draft

An old well-worn work van bounced along the unpaved forest road, jostling the two occupants, and the contents in the back of the van. The dwarf driving the van said little, focused on keeping the interior of the van together as the rough road tried to rattle components loose. Occasionally brushing off the decaying headliner dust off his mechanics jumpsuit that gently rained down from the roof. The passenger, a young looking elf, sat cross-legged in the seat. A look of boredom and mild irritation on their face. Kitt's irritation stemmed from being in this mechanical death trap. It stunk of petrol, sulfurous oil, and clattered loudly as it went over every bump. Worst of all, the vehicle's engine acted liked a magic sinkhole, it's complex workings dulled Kitt's ability to sense the ebb and flow of magic that normally permeated everything.

The road began to curve gently, The dwarf grabbed a clipboard off the dash and without looking at Kitt, handed it to her. "I think we're getting close, read the briefing again. Lets make sure we have a clear idea of what we're getting ourselves into." Kitt took the clipboard and began reading aloud:

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**ARCANE CLEANERS
PROJECT ID: UNDEAD-5736
ASSIGNED TO: SKJÖLD (RANK VETERAN), KITT SHORNLEAVES (RANK RECRUIT)

OBJECTIVE: REMOVE PRINCIPLE OBJECT CAUSING UNDEATH IN GROWING RADIUS AROUND CASTLE LEICHENBLUMEN.

PROJECT NOTES: CITIZENS IN NEIGHBORING VILLAGE NOTE PRESENCE OF UNDEAD PLANTS AND ANIMALS SURROUNDING AN ABANDONED CASTLE IN THE AREA. RADIUS OF UNDEAD MAGIC HAS BEEN SLOWLY INCREASING, VILLAGE OFFICIALS HAVE REQUESTED CLEANING CREW COME IN TO REMOVE/NEUTRALIZE SAID MAGICS FROM AREA. LOCAL REPORTS AND SCOUTING TEAM NOTE NO LIFE SIGNATURES IN OR AROUND CASTLE ASIDE FROM LOCAL WILDLIFE. RECRUIT SHORNLEAVES HAS BEEN ASSIGNED TO TRAINER SKJÖLD AS PART OF HER ON-BOARDING AND TRAINING. TRAINEE FAILURE TO OBSERVE TRAINER ORDERS CAN RESULT IN IMMEDIATE DISMISSAL FROM ARCANE CLEANERS.

SCORE: PREDICTED DIFFICULTY IS A 5/10. TOOLS AND SPELLS ABOVE CATEGORY 3 ARE EXPRESSLY PROHIBITED WITHOUT PRIOR MANAGER APPROVAL.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"That's all that's written, the rest is just photos of the outside of the castle and a map of the area. Are the mission notes always this short?" said Kitt. 

Skjöld looked over, "Unfortunately yes... The project leads run multiple missions at a time, and don't go onsite, so they can't really give us any more info that what the scouts give them. Project scouts get paid per job, not hourly, so their work can best be described as *minimalist.*" Skjöld looked back to the road, it was now straightening out. In the distance, they could now see a large, old castle sitting in the middle of an enormous clearing. As the van crossed from forest to the clearing, Kitt felt the atmosphere suddenly thicken, like someone had suddenly pressed weights against her chest. Her limbs felt heavy, difficult to move, and almost slightly numb, as if she was suddenly wading through cold water. Kitt turned to Skjöld, “did you feel that?”

//add bit about town leadership only paying for service when it starts to affect them

“Yeah, the air feels heavy here, almost like it does deep in a mountain cave. We’re definitely in the right place…” He trailed off, looking ahead as he drove, twisting the steering wheel between his hands. “What I don’t like is that something like this wasn’t in the briefing… It’s not the first time I’ve been given bad notes, but normally they don’t miss something this big.” He continued to look ahead and wring the old wheel in his hands, flakes of old leather from the grip slipping out from under his hands and into his lap. Kitt watched the dwarves gears turn, as he turned leather dust into thoughts. Finally the grinding stopped, and Skjöld glanced over at Kitt. “Just stay sharp on this assignment, okay? Something doesn’t sit right with me on this, and if I’ve learned anything, if it don’t sit right, it probably ain’t right…”

The van’s breaks squealed as it pulled up to the great wooden doors at the front of the stone castle. It made one final complaint before coming to a halt and shuddering into silence, only the quiet ticking of cooling metal coming from the vehicle. Kitt was more than happy to escape out from the decaying metal box. She quickly opened the door and hopped off the passenger seat, inhaling deeply in an effort to force the smell of gasoline and metal shavings out of their nostrils. Skjöld stepped out of the van slowly, scanning the castle from top to bottom. It clearly had not been lived in for decades, and the local flora had taken full advantage of the castle owners absence. Grasses, shrubs, and trees had made their home right up against the weather-worn slabs of stone. His eyes moved down to the castle doors. Twenty feet tall, the massive arching slab of oak and iron towered over the two uninvited guests. The decades had been unkind to the castle, but the doors remained it's stalwart guardian. Skjöld scratched his beard and turned to Kitt, "How good do you think our luck is today?" He walked up to the gate, wrapping his thick gloved hands around one of the massive iron rings. Skjöld braced himself with one foot on the other half of the gate, and gave a massive heave backwards. 

The oak gate was unmoved. 

Skjöld stepped back, dusting off his hands. "Can't say I'm surprised, always worth a shot though. I'm sure I have something in here to open up this door." He walked back to the rear of the van and opened the double doors. Kitt stared at the massive oaken doors, then yelled back towards the van,

"yeah I don't think it'll be that easy. Skjöld come look at this." Kitt stepped towards the gates as Skjöld looked out from behind the van. Taking a deep breath, she touched the door and exhaled, muttering elvish under their breath. As Kitt spoke, etchings lit up with a pale light on the door. The illumination spread from the elf's hand like water down a mountain. Soon the door shone brilliantly in light that shifted back and forth from white to a pale purple. The magical glow revealed what could have been weathering on the face of the door was instead intricately carved words of power, each symbol no bigger than an acorn. The words were arranged in interlocking geometric shapes that spanned from the very top of the gate to where it met the castle threshold. At the center of each gate were a series of sentences in concentric circles, with an enormous rune enclosed within. Skjöld walked up next to Kitt as he read the shimmering words on each door. Kitt turned to Skjöld, "I can't read this, it's not elvish... Can you tell what it says?"

After a couple minutes of scanning the door, Skjöld crossed his arms and turned his head towards Kitt, still not taking his eyes off the door. "Do you want the short or the long version?"

"Start with the short." said Kitt.

"It says, 'No soliciting'." Skjöld said with a smirk.

"Ha ha, good one." Kitt said flatly, making a face to let the dwarf know how well the joke landed. "Okay so give me the long version then."

"I'm only half joking." said Skjöld, turning his head back to the door. He gestured to one of the gate doors, "They're human runes, each one a command. Strung together in sentences strengthens and refines the commands. Inscribing in specific shapes and patterns further enhances their efficacy." The dwarf finally stopped reading the wall, turned, and walked back to the van. "I'm not going to pretend to know everything that's written on these doors, but it's safe to say the magic in that door would withstand anything physical or magic we could throw at it. That gate will still be standing, unbroken, long after we're gone. It'll probably outlast the castle itself. The only way we're getting in today is finding another entrance"

Skjöld opened the rear doors to the van, and began rummaging about. Kitt watched him slowly and methodically pull various tools and equipment out of the van, inspect them, and then notate something on his clipboard. After watching Skjöld in silence for several minutes, Kitt turned, closed her eyes and took a slow, steady breath. Now that she was out of the vehicle, she could better feel the strange magical flow in the area; Kitt scrunched her eyebrows into a frown, eyes still closed. Magic typically flows in all directions at once, Kitt would often describe the sensation like being a rock jutting out of an ocean, with water crashes on you from all sides at once. Here around the castle it was as if there was a sinkhole deep below, with magic flowing from all directions towards a central point somewhere inside. At regular intervals the flow would reverse, flowing outward from the same point, like a surging tide or the breathing of a sleeping giant. The magic flowed more easily through openings in the castle than directly through the stone, and like standing in an ocean tide, Kitt could feel the flow pull her towards the back end of the castle. Kitt opened her eyes and turned to Skjöld, "Hey, I think there may be another entrance on the other side of the castle, do you mind if I go check it out?" Skjöld stopped his inspection, looking up at the elf.

"Sure, knock yourself out. Just remember your vest, if you find something come back and get me, and if you get into trouble pull the red tab." The dwarf returned to his contraptions, stroking his beard, and jotting things down on his clipboard. Kitt reached into the passenger seat and grabbed a standard issue utility vest from underneath the seat. It was tan with reflective hems, plenty of pockets with a variety of tools, and monogrammed with "Arcane Cleaners" in a cursive script on the left breast. What was not standard issue was the small cylindrical pouch with a red pull tab labeled "Pull in Case of Emergency :^)" coming out from under the pouch flap. The emergency pouch was clearly the dwarf's doing, as it was held onto the vest with velcro and what looked like hot glue. Kitt swung the vest onto herself while starting towards the side of the castle. The large vest fit poorly on Kitt's slender frame, and kept trying to slip off her shoulders as she walked along the side of the castle, one arm outstretched to run her nails gently along the stone walls. The elf continued along, following the strange flow of magic as it continued to surge and receed. Idications of the undead magic that permiated the area was everywhere. different parts of the surrounding plants and trees seemed to be stuck in between states of death and regrowth. Fallen tree branches continued to grow, seemingly unbothered by the separation, despite having been dropped long ago and with no roots to sustain it. The shrubs and grasses couldn't make up their mind either; brown and rotting leaves continued to stand firm and sway in the wind, with twisted and dried flowers that still smelled as sweet as the first day they bloomed. Kitt took in this bizarre sight as she rounded the corner of the castle, now reaching the back end of the structure. 

The foliage that encroached on the castle from all sides now grew with impunity on the far end of the castle. Trees and dense shrubs grew right up against the castle walls, some of them starting to sink their roots into the gaps between the stones. Kitt stopped as the plants became too dense to easily walk through and looked around hoping to find another path forward but found none. Closing her eyes again she focused on the unusual magic flow, hoping to glean something her eyes couldn’t see. The elf kept her arms relaxed by her side, turning both her palms towards the castle and fanning her fingers out, like one does as they stand on a beach, feeling the surf flow through their fingers. She could feel the magic rush past her, heading towards the castle. Gently rotating her body left and right, Kitt could feel the flow all pulling towards a central point on the wall. Opening her eyes, she looked back towards the wall where she felt the magic pouring in and then out of the castle wall. Pushing through the brush towards the spot in the castle wall, Kitt came to a couple saplings that were growing out of the stone wall, flat and low against the wall instead of up and away. As Kitt pulled some of the branches aside, she spotted a hole in the wall, no more than three feet round, punched through the stone. Looking through the opening, it didn’t seem like it occurred naturally, but rather an intentionally created one. Looking down, the stones previously part of the wall were all on the outside of the castle, and it looked like they had been carefully pulled away and dragged by hand away from the castle to make space. The young trees also must have been trained to cover the opening into the castle. Kitt turned and scanned the surrounding area, there didn't seem to be anyone around. In this moment, it also occured to her that she hadn't heard much of anything since she got out of the car. Despite the untouched wilderness, she couldn't hear a single bird or animal, as if an entire forest was worried something within it may hear them. Uneasy at this discovery, Kitt turned and started to walk briskly back to the van, her hand no longer following the shape and curves of the stone walls as she had done on her first pass.

Kitt's unease finally started to diminish when the van came back into sight. Skjöld had finished his list making, and he had amassed a small mountain of metal tubes, gears, and other mechanical contraptions onto a low wide wagon looking device. In his hands he held a rectangular metal box with various buttons and dials on it, with a metal antenna on the top. As Skjöld fiddled with the remote, the wagon jerked to life and began slowly moving around the van. The dwarf smirked as Kitt approached, clearly pleased with his contraption. "Well, what do you think dewclaw? It's an automated trolly I made so we don't have to hoof equipment around the castle grounds."

Kitt watched the machine, turning with it as it continued to slowly circle the van, staring as it squeaked and rattled around, trying to figure out what was so impressive about a cart that moved on it's own. "It certainly is something..." Kitt finally said. "Also, who's Dewclaw?"

"Don't play dumb newbie, it's you." Skjöld scoffed, "Let's get to it, what did you find back there, anything worth our time?"

"Yes actually, there's a hole in the wall that looks big enough for us, possibly even that cart if we clear some of the brush and move some of the bigger stones in the way. But there's something weird about it though Skjöld, I'm not sure what to make of it."

Skjöld stopped playing with his mechanical cart and looked at Kitt. "Weird how?"

Kitt furrowed her brows as she recalled the opening, "It doesn't look like it fell apart on its own, or like an animal made it. It looks like something a person, or maybe even multiple people did over a long period of time. I thought the report said there was no one here." Skjöld also furrowed his brows at the news and looked up, thinking. Kitt continued, "Also, have you noticed that there haven’t been any signs of life around the castle since we got here? I can understand the lack of people, but this castle is supposed to be empty for decades, right? Yet there’s no signs of animals anywhere."

Skjöld continued to think wordlessly, stroking his beard and occasionally glancing between the castle and his now motionless cart full of machinery. Finally he looked at Kitt, “You’re right kid, something was already off from the beginning, and it’s only getting worse. From this point on, it’s safe to assume our briefing isn’t worth the paper it’s written on, and frankly, you aren’t supposed to be on missions with an unknown difficulty rating.” Skjöld turned to Kitt, “I’ve never quit a mission, but I don’t want to put you in a situation you’re not ready for.” Skjöld was looking at Kitt, studying her face for any signs of hesitation. “So what’s it going to be dewclaw, are we doing this or not?”
